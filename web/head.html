G-code, simulated.

Paste a CNC milling program or pick an example. A C++ engine checks it for mistakes, works out the cycle time from the machine's acceleration and corner speeds, and plays the toolpath back in 3D. Everything runs in your browser: it's the same code as the command-line tool, compiled to WebAssembly. Works with metric or inch (G20) programs from Fusion 360 and similar CAM, and GRBL routers.

Got a program it reads wrong? Report it with one click: your program and machine are filled in for you.
